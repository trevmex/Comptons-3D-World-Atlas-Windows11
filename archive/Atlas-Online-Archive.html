About Archive Mode

The Internet Archive preserved much of the static 1998 site, but not the live atlas.cgi responses that translated Atlas IDs into context-sensitive pages. Archive Mode opens the local preserved site map for those requests instead of sending insecure traffic to the retired server.

Safety: no AOL client, Internet Explorer, legacy DDE service, plug-in, codec, or system-wide browser setting is installed. The copied pages are read-only historical artifacts; do not run archived installers or supply credentials to archived pages.
